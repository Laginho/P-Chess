#### What to build

BOT-007's script would produce a wrong table for the case Bruno's QA hit (1+0 scrambles). Measured on a 20 MB prefix of `lichess_db_standard_rated_2026-09.pgn.zst` (2026-10-27, 60,764 games):

- **Lichess `%clk` is whole seconds** (0 of 4,049,902 clocks have decimals). In 1+0 under 15% of the clock, the think times are almost all 0, 1, 2 or 3 s; 0 s is 27% of moves at maia-1100 and 46% at maia-1900. The median of those integers is 1 s or the 0.05 s floor, and the log-σ of a mix of ln 0.05 and ln 1 is about 1.5: both wrong.
- **The default 10,000-position uniform reservoir starves the scramble buckets.** 1+0 under 15% is 4.8% of samples, so ~480 positions over 27 buckets (9 levels × 3 top_p bins), under the 50-sample minimum: they all fall back to the formula.

Two fixes:

1. **Fit the log-normal to the clock's rounding.** With a uniformly distributed sub-second phase, a recorded difference `d` (seconds, increment added back) of a true time `t` has likelihood `max(0, 1 − |d − t|)`. Per bucket, fit `(median, log_sigma)` by maximum likelihood over that interval-censored data. Each observation's probability has a closed form from the log-normal CDF and partial expectation, and grouping by integer `d` keeps it fast. Drop the `max(0.05, seconds)` clamp.
2. **Stratify the reservoir** by `(control, level, clock_bin)`, the parts of the key known before Lc0 runs, with a per-stratum limit (`--per-stratum`, default 1,000) replacing `--max-positions`.

Then run the calibration and commit `data/time_model.json`.

#### Acceptance criteria

1. On synthetic samples drawn from a known log-normal and recorded as whole-second clock differences, the fit recovers the median and log-σ within 10% (median 0.6 s σ 0.5, and median 4 s σ 0.8).
2. A bucket of all-zero differences gives a finite median under 0.5 s, not an error.
3. The reservoir keeps up to `--per-stratum` positions in every `(control, level, clock_bin)` stratum, seeded and reproducible.
4. The written table has a bucket for every 1+0 `clock_bin 0` level × top_p bin with at least `--min-samples` samples, and the game loads it without a malformed-table warning.
5. §4.3 documents the whole-second clocks and the fit.
