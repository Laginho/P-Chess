# A Lichess rating is the chess.com rating plus 300

The rating sentence accepts either site, and the app converts with one fixed rule, Lichess = chess.com + 300, instead of a conversion table. Ratings are shown on the chess.com scale; Maia levels, being Lichess ratings, are the converted number clamped to Maia-1's 1100–1900. Bruno chose the flat offset because it is close enough for picking a stage and a Maia level, and a table would need sourcing and upkeep. Don't "fix" it with a table without asking him.
